Description

The problem was never a lack of time to rest. It's that none of our rest works.
Scrolling doesn't create distance from the day — you can worry and scroll at the same time.
Lying still invites the thoughts back in. Meditation asks beginners to stop thinking, which is precisely what they can't do. And the physical activities that would work demand a partner, a court, daylight, or a change of clothes — none of which exist at eleven at night, when recovery is needed most.

So the choice narrows to two bad options: something still that can't quiet the mind, or something active that asks for performance and preparation. RALLY is the missing third.

The App

Glass and Blade play the rally. The app decides what kind of rally you need tonight.

Open it, and it asks nothing about your form or your goals — only how the day went. From there it sets the tempo, the length of the rally, and how much room it leaves for your mind to wander without ever asking you to sit still and try not to.

01

Tempo from mood

A short check-in sets rally speed — slow and long after a hard day, quick and short when you're wired.

02

Room-aware play

Rally maps your space once and keeps the game inside it — a bedroom corner is enough court.

03

Rally streak, not score

The only number the app keeps is consecutive nights played — never a score to chase or beat.
